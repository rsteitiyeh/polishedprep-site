Arizona cosmetology license renewal costs $60 for a personal license, renewed online through the Arizona Board of Barbering and Cosmetology (BCB). That $60 figure covers cosmetology, esthetics, and nail technology licenses alike under the board's current fee schedule; barber licenses run a different fee structure entirely, at $80 to renew.

Below is the full fee table straight from the board's own page, plus what changes if you let a license go delinquent.

## What does Arizona charge to renew a cosmetology license?

$60, flat, for a personal cosmetology, esthetics, or nail technology license, according to the Arizona Board of Barbering and Cosmetology's current fee schedule ([bcb.az.gov/resources/fees](https://bcb.az.gov/resources/fees)). That's the same figure as the initial license fee for these categories, which is unusual; many states charge more to renew than to first apply.

## How does that compare to barber license fees?

It's noticeably different. Barber personal licenses run $40 to initially apply but jump to $80 to renew, nearly the reverse pattern of the cosmetology/esthetics/nail technology fee structure, where the initial and renewal fees match at $60. If you hold a barber license alongside a cosmetology-track license, budget for two different renewal amounts, not one flat number across both.

## Arizona's full personal license fee schedule

| License type | Initial | Renewal | Delinquent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosmetology / Esthetics / Nail Technology | $60 | $60 | $30 |
| Barber | $40 | $80 | $25 |

Source: Arizona Board of Barbering and Cosmetology fee page, current as of July 2026 ([bcb.az.gov/resources/fees](https://bcb.az.gov/resources/fees)).

## What does the delinquent fee add on top of a late renewal?

An extra $30 for cosmetology, esthetics, and nail technology licenses, or $25 for barber licenses, charged on top of the standard renewal amount once a license moves into delinquent status. The board's fee page lists this as a separate line item from the base renewal fee, so a delinquent cosmetology renewal totals $90 ($60 renewal plus $30 delinquent), not just the $30 penalty alone.

## What does the exam itself cost in Arizona, separate from renewal?

If you're licensing for the first time rather than renewing, Arizona's written exam runs $100 and the practical exam runs $77, both paid to the board's third-party testing vendor rather than to the board directly ([bcb.az.gov/resources/fees](https://bcb.az.gov/resources/fees)). That's separate from, and in addition to, the $60 initial personal license fee. Renewal doesn't require retaking either exam; those fees only apply at initial licensure or after a lapse serious enough to require re-testing.

## Does Arizona require continuing education to renew?

The board's published fee schedule doesn't list a continuing education requirement as part of routine renewal for cosmetology, esthetics, or nail technology personal licenses. If your renewal notice or online portal shows a CE requirement specific to your license history, follow what the board's system tells you directly rather than a general rule, since fee and requirement pages update independently of each other and a site like this one can't catch every mid-cycle change the day it happens.

## How do you actually renew online?

Through the Arizona Board of Barbering and Cosmetology's online licensing portal, using your license number to look up your specific renewal window and current fee. The board's renewal page is the authoritative source for your license's exact due date; a birth-month or issuance-date renewal cycle is common among state boards generally, but confirm your specific date through the portal rather than assuming it matches a pattern from another state.

## What about establishment (salon) license renewal in Arizona?

That's a separate fee category from your personal license. Arizona's establishment renewal fee runs $50 for cosmetology, esthetics, and nail technology establishments, and also $50 for barber establishments, distinct from both the $60 personal cosmetology renewal and the $80 personal barber renewal ([bcb.az.gov/resources/fees](https://bcb.az.gov/resources/fees)). If you own the salon you work in, you're renewing two separate licenses on potentially two separate schedules, not one combined fee.

## How does Arizona's fee compare to nearby states?

It's roughly in the middle of what we've checked. Oklahoma charges $40 per year (comparable to $80 across a 2-year span), Colorado charges $40 per 2-year cycle, and Tennessee charges $60 per 2-year cycle, matching Arizona exactly. Arizona's $60 flat rate for cosmetology, esthetics, and nail technology, with no continuing education requirement listed, sits on the lighter-to-middle end of the states in this batch rather than at either extreme.

## FAQ

**How much does it cost to renew a cosmetology license in Arizona?**
$60 for a personal cosmetology, esthetics, or nail technology license, according to the Arizona Board of Barbering and Cosmetology's current fee page. Barber renewal costs $80, a different amount.

**Is the renewal fee the same as the initial license fee in Arizona?**
For cosmetology, esthetics, and nail technology, yes, both are $60. Barber licenses differ: $40 to initially apply but $80 to renew.

**What happens if I renew my Arizona license late?**
A $30 delinquent fee applies on top of the $60 renewal for cosmetology, esthetics, and nail technology licenses ($25 on top of $80 for barber licenses), based on the board's published fee schedule.

**Can I renew my Arizona cosmetology license online?**
Yes, through the Arizona Board of Barbering and Cosmetology's online licensing portal, using your license number.

**Does Arizona require continuing education to renew a cosmetology license?**
The board's fee schedule doesn't list a general CE requirement for routine renewal of these license types. Check your specific renewal notice through the board's portal for anything that applies to your individual license history.
